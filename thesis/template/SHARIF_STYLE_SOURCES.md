# English Sharif-style presentation

The requested Sharif LaTeX and Overleaf template pages could not be retrieved through the environment proxy (HTTP 403). The public [Sharif thesis template by Behnam Momeni](https://github.com/momeni/sharif-thesis) was retrieved and inspected instead, including its English title page, `sharifthesis.cls`, and the included Central Library guide `resources/Guide_Theses-98.pdf`.

This manuscript uses an English-only adaptation of the visual structure. It does not load the Persian XeLaTeX class. The title page places the English university seal above the university and department, followed by the degree, title, author and supervisor. Chapter headings are centered and black; section headings, citations and hyperlinks are black. The A4 margins follow the inspected class with the binding margin mirrored to the left for English. Times-family text is supplied by `mathptmx`; this is a compatible Times-style font, not a bundled Microsoft Times New Roman font. Scientific plot text uses the matching STIX serif family in black while retaining data colors.

Submission and document dates are omitted. Publication years remain in bibliographic entries. The final PDF omits creation and modification timestamps.

The English university seal at `assets/sharif-logo-en.pdf` is an unmodified copy of `img/logo-en.pdf` in the inspected upstream repository. The upstream license is included verbatim at `assets/SHARIF_LOGO_LICENSE.txt`; no upstream class code has been copied into the manuscript. The exact commit and asset checksum are recorded in `generated/formatting_provenance.json`. The university emblem is used to identify the university on this thesis cover.

The original uploaded example remains under `template/` for historical reference and is not included as manuscript text.
